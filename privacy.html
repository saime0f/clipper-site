Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Clipper ("the app") is a personal tool operated by its owner for their own use. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • TikTok (via Login Kit and the Content Posting API): basic profile information (display name, username, avatar) and the creator settings returned by TikTok, used only to show who is posting and which privacy options are available; permission to upload videos the owner chooses to publish.
  • YouTube (via the YouTube Data API): the channel name, used to show which channel is connected; permission to upload videos the owner chooses to publish.
  • Instagram (via the Instagram API with Instagram Login): the username and account ID, and permission to publish Reels the owner chooses to publish.

How data is stored

Access tokens, refresh tokens and app settings are stored only in a local database on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any server other than the official API endpoints of TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta/Instagram, and only to perform actions the owner initiated.

The app does not collect analytics, does not use advertising, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not access data of any user other than the owner.

Videos

Videos and transcripts are processed and stored locally on the owner's computer. A video is uploaded to a platform only when the owner presses "Publish" for that platform.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect any account in the app settings, which deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the TikTok, Google or Instagram account settings. Deleting the app folder removes all locally stored data.
