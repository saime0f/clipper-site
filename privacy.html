This policy explains what data Clipper accesses, why, and how you can delete it.

What Clipper is

Clipper is a self-hosted app. It runs on the owner’s own computer and is used from a phone over a private network. The owner may invite a few people by email; each invited person has a separate account with their own projects and their own connected social accounts.

Data Clipper accesses

  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): your display name, username and avatar, and the creator settings TikTok returns, to show who is posting and which privacy options you can choose; permission to upload the videos you publish.
  • YouTube (YouTube Data API): your channel name, to show which channel is connected; permission to upload the videos you publish.
  • Instagram (Instagram API with Instagram Login): your username and account ID, and permission to publish the Reels you publish.
  • Your Clipper account: your email address and a hashed password, used only to sign you in and to send a password reset link.

Where data is kept

Access tokens, refresh tokens and settings are kept only in a local database on the owner’s computer. They are sent only to the official endpoints of TikTok, Google and Meta, and only to do something you started, such as publishing a clip.

Clipper has no central server, collects no analytics and shows no advertising.

Sharing

Clipper does not sell or share your data. If you choose to post through BrightBean in Settings, the clip, its caption and the accounts you pick are sent to BrightBean so it can publish them; that is covered by BrightBean’s own privacy policy.

Videos

Videos and transcripts are processed and stored on the owner’s computer. A clip leaves it only when you press Publish or schedule it.

Deleting your data

Disconnect an account in Settings to delete its tokens. You can also remove access in TikTok, Google or Instagram account settings at any time. To delete your Clipper account and all its files, write to ogoodnews.daily@gmail.com; it is done within 7 days.

Children

Clipper is not meant for anyone under 18.

Contact

Questions about this policy: ogoodnews.daily@gmail.com.
