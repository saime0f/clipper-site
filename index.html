Clipper

Clipper is a personal, self-hosted tool that turns long videos into short vertical clips (9:16) with burned-in subtitles, and lets its owner publish those clips to their own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

How it works

  1. The owner adds a video (a link or a file). Clipper transcribes the speech and cuts the video into 70–95 second clips.
  2. Each clip gets subtitles and a background gameplay video in the bottom half. The owner can edit subtitles, titles and descriptions.
  3. When the owner chooses to publish, Clipper uploads the clip to the account the owner connected, with the title, description and privacy settings the owner selected on the publish screen.

Who can use it

Clipper runs on the owner's own computer and is accessible only to the owner over a private network. It is not a public service and has no other users.
