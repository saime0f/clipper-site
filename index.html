Your TikTok account, your call

What Clipper asks for, and why.

  • Log in with TikTok

    You connect your own account with Login Kit. Clipper reads your display name and avatar (user.info.basic) to show who is posting.

  • Post what you approve

    Clipper uploads only the clips you choose, with the caption and privacy setting you pick on the publish screen (video.publish).

  • Kept on your computer

    Access tokens stay in a database on your own PC. There is no Clipper server and no analytics.

  • Disconnect any time

    One tap in Settings deletes the tokens. You can also remove access in TikTok under Security and permissions.
